Reckonry Timer for Karbon

A stopwatch for your Karbon timer that follows you around the browser. Start, pause, and switch timers from any site, and let the extension handle the forgettable parts.

Set up in three steps

  1. 1
    Pin the stopwatch to your toolbar
    Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin beside Reckonry Timer for Karbon. The badge then shows your running time on every site.
    Pick the icon color that's easiest to see on your toolbar
  2. 2
    Connect to Karbon
    The extension uses the Karbon sign-in already in this browser. If you're signed in, this step finishes on its own.
  3. 3
    Choose your preferences
    Click the stopwatch, then open Preferences. When you open a work item with no timer running, choose to auto-start a timer (the default, with a 2-second countdown you can cancel), get a reminder, or do nothing. Make the same choice for contacts, which do nothing by default. Auto-switch, which moves a running timer to the item you open, is off until you turn it on.

What it does

  • Start from anywhere. Press play with nothing chosen, or search work items and contacts, or pick a recent item, without leaving the page you're on. A timer started without a work item asks for one when you stop it.
  • Follow your work. Open a work item and the timer starts after a short countdown you can cancel. Optionally do the same for contacts, switch a running timer to what you open, or get a reminder instead.
  • Pause when you step away. Locking your screen, going idle, or putting the computer to sleep pauses the timer and records why.
  • Land time on the right day. A timer running at 11:59 PM is saved and restarted just after midnight. A timer with no work item yet is paused instead, and a notification asks you to finish it.
  • Catch forgotten timers. The badge turns yellow, and you get an alert, once a timer runs past your limit.

About notes on time entries

The extension adds short, time-stamped notes to the entry description, such as "Timer paused: screen locked." Descriptions can appear on timesheets and invoices, so notes never name other work items or clients. You can turn notes off, or limit them to automatic actions, under Preferences, then Time entries.

Privacy

  • The extension talks only to Karbon, using the session you're already signed in with. It never sees or stores your password.
  • Settings and the last timer state stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and there is no tracking.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Alt+Shift+K starts, pauses, or resumes the timer from any site. With no timer running, it starts one with no work item; you choose it when you stop.
  • Alt+Shift+T opens the stopwatch panel.
  • Change either one at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
