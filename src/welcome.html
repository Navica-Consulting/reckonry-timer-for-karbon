Get started

A stopwatch for your Karbon timer that follows you around the browser. Start, pause, and switch timers from any site, and let the extension handle the forgettable parts.

Changes save as you make them, and sync to your other computers if Chrome Sync is on. The stopwatch's Preferences has Work, Clients, and Auto-switch for quick changes.

How it works

The extension drives the Karbon timer you already use. Every entry it saves is a normal Karbon time entry.

Start from anywhere

Click the stopwatch on any site. Search work items and clients, pick a recent one, or press play with nothing chosen. If Karbon isn't open, it opens in a background tab when an action needs it.

Follow your work

Open a work item in Karbon and the timer starts after a short countdown in the corner of the page. Press Cancel, or move on, and nothing happens. With a timer already running, auto-switch saves the current entry and moves the timer to what you opened.

Timers with no work item

Start a timer before you know what it's for. When you stop it, a short form asks for the work item, plus a role, task type, or notes if your firm requires them. You can save, keep it paused, or discard it.

While it runs, opening a work item or client shows a pop-up that stays until you answer: finish the timer there, or keep it running.

Stepping away

Locking your screen or going idle can pause the timer, and coming back can resume it. Chrome can't act while the computer sleeps, so time asleep stays on the timer. When it wakes, the timer pauses and a note gives the time away, so you can trim the entry.

The badge

  • 25m A timer is running, with its time.
  • II The timer is paused.
  • 3h05 It has run past your long-timer limit.
  • ! A stopped timer still needs its details. Click the stopwatch to finish it.
  • off No timer is running.

About notes on time entries

The extension adds short, time-stamped notes to the entry description, such as "Timer paused: screen locked." Descriptions can appear on timesheets and invoices, so notes never name other work items or clients. You can turn notes off, or limit them to automatic actions, under Time entries.

Questions

Why did a timer start on its own?

Auto-start is on for work items by default. When you open one with no timer running, a 5-second countdown shows in the corner of the Karbon page. Press Cancel to stop it, or choose Remind me or Do nothing under Work.

Why didn't my timer switch when I opened a work item?

Auto-switch may be off for that kind of item. Clients are off by default. It also waits for the countdown, so leaving the page or cancelling stops it, and it only acts in the Karbon tab you're looking at.

A timer with no work item never switches. A pop-up asks you to finish it first.

How do I finish a timer with no work item?

Stop it from the stopwatch, and a short form asks for the work item and anything else your firm requires. If the badge shows a yellow "!", a stopped timer is waiting there for its details.

Do I need to keep a Karbon tab open?

No. The stopwatch shows your timer without one. Starting, pausing, searching, and saving use Karbon, so the extension opens it in a background tab when needed. Auto-start and auto-switch work on the Karbon pages you open.

Will notes show up on invoices?

They can. Notes go in the entry's description, and descriptions can appear on timesheets, invoices, and reports. That's why notes never name other work items or clients. Turn them off, or limit them to automatic actions, under Time entries.

Why was my timer split or paused at midnight?

So each day's time lands on the right day. The timer is saved at 11:59 PM and restarted just after midnight. A timer with no work item can't be saved on its own, so it's paused and keeps its date until you finish it. Turn this off under Time entries.

My firm books time to work items only. What changes?

The extension reads this setting from Karbon. Clients still have their own choice, but a timer started from a client has no work item, and the finish form asks for one when you stop.

Does the extension see my password or client data?

It uses the Karbon sign-in already in your browser and never sees your password. It talks only to Karbon. Settings and timer state stay in Chrome, and settings sync to your Google account if Chrome Sync is on. There is no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Where are the rest of the settings in the stopwatch?

The stopwatch keeps Work, Clients, and Auto-switch, the ones people change most. Everything else is on this page. Changes in either place show in both.
