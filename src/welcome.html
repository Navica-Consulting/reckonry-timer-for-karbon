Reckonry Timer for Karbon

A stopwatch for your Karbon timer that follows you around the browser. Start, pause, and switch timers from any site, and let the extension handle the forgettable parts.

Set up in four steps

  1. 1
    Pin the stopwatch to your toolbar
    Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin beside Reckonry Timer for Karbon. The badge then shows your running time on every site.
  2. 2
    Open Karbon and sign in
    The extension works through your own Karbon session. It learns your workspace the first time Karbon loads.
  3. 3
    Start one timer in Karbon by hand
    This teaches the extension your usual role and task type, which it uses for every timer it starts. You can stop and discard that timer right away.
  4. 4
    Choose your preferences
    Click the stopwatch, then open Preferences. Auto-start on work items is on, with a 2-second countdown you can cancel. Auto-start on contacts, auto-switch, and reminders are off until you turn them on.

What it does

  • Start from anywhere. Search work items and contacts, pick a recent item, or use your default work item, without leaving the page you're on.
  • Follow your work. Open a work item and the timer starts after a short countdown you can cancel. Optionally do the same for contacts, switch a running timer to what you open, or get a reminder instead.
  • Pause when you step away. Locking your screen, going idle, or putting the computer to sleep pauses the timer and records why.
  • Land time on the right day. A timer running at 11:59 PM is saved and restarted just after midnight.
  • Catch forgotten timers. The badge turns yellow, and you get an alert, once a timer runs past your limit.

About notes on time entries

The extension adds short, time-stamped notes to the entry description, such as "Timer paused: screen locked." Descriptions can appear on timesheets and invoices, so notes never name other work items or clients. You can turn notes off, or limit them to automatic actions, under Preferences, then Logging.

Privacy

  • The extension talks only to Karbon, using the session you're already signed in with. It never sees or stores your password.
  • Settings and the last timer state stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and there is no tracking.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Alt+Shift+K pauses or resumes the timer from any site.
  • Alt+Shift+T opens the stopwatch panel.
  • Change either one at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
