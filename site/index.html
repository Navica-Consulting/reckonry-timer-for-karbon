Why it exists

Most lost time isn't lost. It's just never logged.

Karbon's timer is good. The hard part is remembering to use it while you jump between emails, clients, and calls all day.

Forgot to start

You dive into a return and look up an hour later. The timer never ran.

Forgot to switch

Forty minutes on one client end up on another, because the timer stayed where it was.

Forgot to stop

A timer runs through lunch, a meeting, or the night, and someone has to fix it later.

Rebuilt at 5 PM

The day gets pieced together from memory and the inbox. Short tasks never make it in.

How it works

A stopwatch for Karbon, in your toolbar.

It works through Karbon's own timer, so every entry is a normal Karbon time entry. Your firm's roles, task types, and rules all still apply.

Open new work, and the timer follows.

Open a work item and the timer starts after a short countdown. Open a different one and it saves the current entry and switches.

Just looking? Click Cancel during the countdown and nothing changes.

Start from any tab in two clicks.

Pick a client, and Work lists that client's recent jobs. You don't need a Karbon tab open.

Not sure yet? Start a blank timer and choose the work when you stop.

Presets for the stages of a job.

Save a role and task type as a one-click button, such as Prep, Client meeting, and Review.

Click a preset's name to change the running timer. Click its swap icon to save this entry and start the next stage.

No time entry left half done.

When Karbon needs more before it can save, the timer pauses and asks. Anything required is outlined in red.

Save, and the timer moves on to your new work.

Pauses when you step away.

Lock your screen, and the timer pauses. It resumes when you're back and adds a short note about the gap.

Idle pause is one switch away, for people who step away without locking.

Use cases

Built for the way firms actually work.

A few common days, and how the extension fits into them.

The bookkeeper

Fifteen clients before lunch.

Month-end close means bank feeds, receipts, and quick questions across a long client list. Switching the timer by hand for each one is where time gets lost.

With auto-switch, opening the next client's work item moves the timer there. A four-second countdown means a quick peek doesn't count.

The advisor

One meeting, three kinds of time.

An advisory session has prep, the meeting itself, and follow-up. Your firm wants each logged with its own task type, so it can see where the hours go.

Presets make each stage one click. The work item stays the same, and only the role and task type change.

The partner

Back-to-back calls, no time to click.

Between client calls, reviews, and hallway questions, the timer is the last thing on your mind. Then a timer runs over lunch, or overnight.

Lock pause catches the lunch break. A long-timer alert flags anything past the hours you set. At midnight the timer splits, so each day's time lands on the right day.

The firm owner

Better numbers without chasing anyone.

Budgets, realization, and pricing decisions all depend on time data. Chasing the team for timesheets every Friday doesn't scale.

When the timer runs itself, entries arrive during the day, on the right work item, with role and task type filled in by your firm's own rules.

These are typical scenarios, not quotes from specific firms.

Everything else

Small things that add up.

Clients too

If your firm books time to people, organizations, or client groups, choose auto-start, a reminder, or nothing for those pages.

Smart fill-in

Role and task type fill in the way Karbon's own timer does, following your firm's settings.

Edit as you go

Change the work item, role, task type, or notes on a running timer right from the panel.

Clear notes

Automatic actions add a short, time-stamped note, such as "Timer paused: screen locked." Notes never name other work items or clients.

Timer badge

See your running time on the toolbar icon, everywhere or only when you are away from Karbon.

Keyboard shortcuts

Alt+Shift+K starts, pauses, or resumes the timer. Alt+Shift+T opens the panel.

Questions

Common questions.

Can I install it?

No. Karbon told us the extension goes against its terms, so we pulled it from the Chrome Web Store. Please don't run a copy of it. This page stays up to show what's possible. If you'd like Karbon to offer something like it, vote for the feature idea in the Loft.

Will it log time I didn't mean to?

Every automatic start or switch shows a countdown first, with a Cancel button. You can turn any automatic action off, or switch it to a reminder instead.

Does my whole firm need to use it?

No. It was built so each person installed it in their own Chrome. Everyone else kept using Karbon as usual, and every entry was a normal Karbon time entry.

Is it made by Karbon?

No. Reckonry Timer for Karbon is an independent product built by Navica LLC. It is not made, endorsed, or supported by Karbon.

How do I get help or send feedback?

Open the extension's settings page and choose Send feedback. It fills in your version and settings, and you choose what to include.
