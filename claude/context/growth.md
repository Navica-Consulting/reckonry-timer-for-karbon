# Growth and permission marketing

How the extension earns attention and contact details from users.

## Approach
- 2026-10-22: Use a permission marketing approach: small in-extension prompts, an email invite with opt-out of reminders, and, after about a week of automatic time entries, a feedback request with a Navica newsletter invite (Max).

- 2026-10-24: Feedback goes to a Google Form, not email, with an optional reply email. Responses land in the form's Sheet. Claude suggested two separate opt-ins on the form (breakage alerts, Navica newsletter); not added yet (Max).
- 2026-10-24: Emails from the feedback form are never used for marketing. No newsletter opt-in on the form (Max).
- 2026-10-24: No breakage-alert mailing list; there's no bandwidth or infrastructure to run one. The form's email is for replies only (Max).

## Data boundaries
- 2026-10-22: Collect only email addresses, never mailbox content (Max).
